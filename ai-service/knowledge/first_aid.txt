First Aid Basics - Educational Overview
For minor cuts: clean the wound with running water, apply gentle pressure with a clean
cloth to stop bleeding, and cover with a sterile bandage. For minor burns: cool the area
under running water for 10-20 minutes, do not apply ice directly, and cover loosely.
For choking in a conscious adult: encourage coughing first; if unable to breathe, abdominal
thrusts (Heimlich maneuver) may be used by a trained person. For suspected fractures,
sprains, severe bleeding, chest pain, or difficulty breathing, this is a medical emergency
and professional emergency services should be contacted immediately. This information is
educational only and does not replace certified first-aid training or professional care.
