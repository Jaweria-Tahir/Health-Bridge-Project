Hypertension (High Blood Pressure) - Prevention Basics
Hypertension means the force of blood against artery walls is consistently too high.
Key prevention steps: reduce salt intake to under 5g per day, exercise at least 30 minutes
most days of the week, maintain a healthy body weight, limit alcohol, avoid smoking,
manage stress through sleep and relaxation, and eat a diet rich in fruits, vegetables,
and whole grains (often called the DASH diet). Routine blood pressure checks are
recommended for adults, especially those with a family history of hypertension.
Warning signs of a hypertensive crisis (severe headache, chest pain, vision problems)
require immediate emergency medical attention, not self-treatment.
