Personal Hygiene - Preventive Care
Good hygiene habits reduce the spread of infections. Core practices: wash hands with
soap for at least 20 seconds before eating and after using the toilet, bathe regularly,
brush teeth twice daily, keep wounds clean and covered, wash fruits and vegetables before
eating, and ensure safe drinking water. In community settings, proper handwashing is one
of the single most effective ways to reduce the spread of diarrheal and respiratory
illness. Handwashing stations and clean water access are considered basic public health
infrastructure.
