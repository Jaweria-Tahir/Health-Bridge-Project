Nutrition Basics - Preventive Health
A balanced diet supports immune function and reduces risk of chronic disease. General
guidance: include a variety of fruits and vegetables daily, choose whole grains over
refined grains, include protein sources such as legumes, fish, eggs, or lean meat, limit
added sugar and highly processed foods, limit sodium, and drink adequate water daily.
Portion control and consistent meal timing also support stable energy and metabolic
health. Specific dietary needs vary by age, activity level, and existing health
conditions, so individualized plans should involve a nutrition professional.
