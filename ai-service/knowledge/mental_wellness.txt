Mental Wellness - Community Resources Overview
Mental wellness includes emotional, psychological, and social well-being. Everyday
supportive practices include regular sleep, physical activity, staying socially
connected, setting realistic goals, and taking breaks from stress. Recognizing early
signs of distress, such as persistent low mood, withdrawal from activities, or
difficulty functioning day-to-day, is important. Community mental health resources
(counseling centers, helplines, support groups) can provide support. Anyone in crisis
or having thoughts of self-harm should be connected to emergency services or a crisis
helpline immediately, not handled through self-help content alone.
